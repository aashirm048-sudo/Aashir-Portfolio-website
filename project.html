Why this matters

Maintenance problems are often hidden in fragmented processes.

Fragmented reporting

Issues reach teams through chat, calls, paper notes, or forgotten tickets.

No shared record

Assets lack a consistent digital footprint, making accountability hard to track.

Reactive maintenance

Small problems become costly failures because preventive action is delayed.

Platform capabilities

MaintainIQ is more than a QR code entry point.

Digital asset identity

Every asset gets a permanent profile with location, type, owners, criticality, and lifecycle details.

Public asset page

QR scanning opens a public page for issue reporting, status visibility, and service context.

Issue triage workflow

Reports are categorized, prioritized, assigned, and tracked through to resolution.

Evidence and history

Photos, notes, technician handoffs, and service logs create a durable audit trail.

Preventive recommendations

Historical patterns support alerts, recurring checks, and smarter maintenance planning.

Accountability

Every action, owner, and outcome is visible so nothing falls through the cracks.

How it works

A clean flow from report to resolution.

01

Scan or identify asset

The user opens the asset page through a QR code, tag, or search.

02

Report an issue

They submit a description, severity, image evidence, and location context.

03

Triage and assign

The maintenance team reviews the case, prioritizes it, and deploys the right technician.

04

Resolve and record

Work is completed, evidence is stored, and the asset history is updated permanently.
